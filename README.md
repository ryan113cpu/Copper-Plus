## Copper Plus
Copper Plus is a small Fabric mod that adds useful copper-based items to Minecraft.

## Copper Wire
Copper Wire is used to craft the Copper Magnet.

## Copper Magnet
The Copper Magnet pulls nearby dropped items toward the player from up to 8 blocks away.

Right-click the magnet to turn it ON or OFF. The item tooltip also shows whether the magnet is currently ON or OFF.

## Features
Copper Wire
Copper Magnet
8-block item attraction
Magnet ON/OFF toggle
ON/OFF tooltip
Crafting recipes for both items
Made for Fabric.
